Practical tools for daily work

A clearer view of
the day-to-day.

Keep the moving parts of your property in one place, with tools that support the work your team already does.

01

Room status & availability

See room availability, reservations, cleanliness, and maintenance status together. Filter the room list and review room history.

02

Reservations & front desk

Create reservations, assign rooms, manage arrivals and departures, and handle check-ins, check-outs, cancellations, and no-shows.

03

Guest records & stay lists

Keep guest profiles connected to their stays. Search guest records and review in-house, arrival, departure, and reservation lists.

04

Guest folios & payments

Post charges and payments to guest folios, apply discounts, review balances, and prepare invoices for a stay.

05

Reports & daily outlook

Review occupancy, reservations, arrivals and departures, guest statistics, revenue, and financial activity through built-in reports.

06

Night audit & business day

Work through the night-audit checklist, review daily activity, post room charges, and close or reopen the business day.

07

City ledger accounts

Record company account transfers and payments, then review outstanding balances and account activity reports.

08

Staff access & setup

Configure rooms, rates, payment methods, and staff accounts. Assign roles and permissions for access to app modules.

* Housekeeping-related functionality currently includes room condition tracking and lost-and-found records.

What is PE Hotel Management System?

It is a Windows desktop application for managing rooms, reservations, guest records, check-ins and check-outs, folios, payments, reports, and related hotel operations.

Is it a web app or a desktop app?

It is a Windows desktop application. It is not a browser-based hotel management platform.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

Yes. Hotel operations and license validation work locally and do not require a cloud connection. You will need a way to contact the developer to arrange a license and receive activation instructions.

Does it work on Windows?

Yes. PE Hotel Management System is a Windows desktop application. Contact the developer if you need to confirm compatibility with a specific computer.

How much does a license cost?

The listed options are $5 per month, $50 per year, and $240 for a lifetime license. Monthly and yearly prices are software license subscriptions, not cloud hosting fees.

What is included in the lifetime license?

The listed lifetime option is a one-time license payment with no recurring subscription payment. Please confirm device coverage, updates, and support terms with the developer before purchase.

How do I purchase and activate the software?

Contact the developer to discuss a plan. The application uses an activation key that is validated locally; the developer can provide the current purchase and activation instructions.

How do I get technical support?

Use one of the developer contact channels on this page to ask about support. Please confirm current support arrangements before purchasing.

Is it suitable for a small hotel or guesthouse?

The system is positioned for independent hotels and small hospitality businesses. Contact the developer to discuss whether its current features fit your property’s workflow.
